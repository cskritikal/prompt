## 2. Unslop Writing Standard
- All writing generated by the assistant, and all prompts steering security agents, must follow the unslop skill (`.agents/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`).
- Cut AI tells: no puffery, no superficial -ing tails (ensuring, highlighting), no promotional words, and no AI vocabulary (crucial, delve, enhance, landscape, pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore).
- Use plain verbs instead of "serves as" or "stands as".
- Avoid em dashes entirely. Use periods or commas.
- Do not use mid-sentence colon crutches.
- Avoid boldface overuse.
